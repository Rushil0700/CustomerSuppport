# Downgrade to a lower plan (Starter plan)

## Symptom

A customer wants to reduce spend.

## Cause

Downgrades take effect at the end of the paid period so the customer keeps what they paid for.

## Resolution

This article covers the **Starter** plan ($12 per user per month, 3 seats included).

1. Settings > Billing > **Change plan** and select the lower plan.
2. Read the warning: seats above the new limit are deactivated, and storage above the new limit becomes read-only.
3. Remove excess members and data first if you want to avoid that.
4. Confirm. The change is scheduled for the renewal date and can be cancelled until then.

## Verify

Billing shows 'Scheduled: downgrade to <plan> on <date>'.

## When to escalate

Escalate if the customer needs a mid-period downgrade with a refund.
