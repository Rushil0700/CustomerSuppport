# Upgrade to a higher plan (Team plan)

## Symptom

The workspace has hit a seat, storage or feature limit.

## Cause

Limits are per plan and take effect immediately on upgrade.

## Resolution

This article covers the **Team** plan ($29 per user per month, 25 seats included).

1. Settings > Billing > **Change plan**.
2. Pick the plan and the billing period; annual saves two months.
3. Review the prorated amount - you are charged only for the remainder of the current period.
4. Confirm. New limits apply within seconds.

## Verify

Settings > Usage shows the new limits and the invoice appears under Billing history.

## When to escalate

Escalate when an upgrade is charged but the limits do not change within five minutes.
