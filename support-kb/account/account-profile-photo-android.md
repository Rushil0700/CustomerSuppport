# Change your profile photo or display name (the Android app)

## Symptom

A member wants to update how they appear to colleagues.

## Cause

Profile fields are per account and shared across every workspace you belong to.

## Resolution

These steps are written for **the Android app**. Use Settings > Apps > Acme Cloud > Storage > Clear cache (not Clear data).

1. Settings > Profile.
2. Upload a square image of at least 256 by 256 pixels; larger images are cropped centrally.
3. Edit the display name. It appears on comments, shares and the audit log.
4. Where SSO is enforced, name and photo come from the identity provider and are read-only here.

## Verify

The new photo and name appear on your own comments after a refresh.

## When to escalate

Rarely needed.
