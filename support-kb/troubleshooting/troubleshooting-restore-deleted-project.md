# Restore a deleted project

## Symptom

A whole project has gone.

## Cause

Deleted by an admin; recoverable for 30 days.

## Resolution

1. Settings > Workspace > **Trash** > Projects.
2. Find the project and choose **Restore**. Members and permissions come back with it.
3. Check Activity to see who deleted it and when - worth knowing before it happens twice.
4. Beyond 30 days the project is purged and cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

## Verify

The project appears in the list with its files and members intact.

## When to escalate

Escalate immediately if the 30 day window is close to expiring - there is nothing anyone can do afterwards.
