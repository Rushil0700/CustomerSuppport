# Invite your team on the web app

## Steps

1. Settings > Members > **Invite**.
2. Paste addresses separated by commas and pick a role for each.
3. Members see every project; guests see only what you add them to.
4. Invitations expire after 7 days and can be resent.

## Platform note

Clear the site data for app.acme.example under your browser's privacy settings.

## Verify

Each invitee appears as Pending and flips to Active on acceptance.
