# Download an invoice or receipt (Enterprise plan)

## Symptom

Finance needs a PDF invoice for expense or tax purposes.

## Cause

Every charge produces an invoice available indefinitely to admins and owners.

## Resolution

This article covers the **Enterprise** plan (custom per user per month, unlimited seats included).

1. Settings > Billing > **Billing history**.
2. Choose the period and select **Download PDF**.
3. To have invoices emailed automatically, add a billing email under Billing > Contacts.

## Verify

The PDF carries the invoice number, VAT/GST treatment and the workspace's billing address.

## When to escalate

Escalate for invoices predating a workspace migration, which may not be self-serve.
