# Pay by invoice instead of card

## Symptom

A finance team cannot use a company card.

## Cause

Invoice billing (net 30) is available on Business and Enterprise annual plans.

## Resolution

1. Contact support or your account manager with the workspace name and a purchase order if required.
2. We issue an invoice with bank details and a 30 day term.
3. Once the first invoice is settled, the workspace switches to invoice billing for the term.

## Verify

Settings > Billing shows 'Invoice (net 30)' as the payment method.

## When to escalate

Always handled by a human: invoice terms are a contractual change.
