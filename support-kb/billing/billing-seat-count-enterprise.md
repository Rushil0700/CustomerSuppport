# Understand how seats are counted and billed (Enterprise plan)

## Symptom

The invoice total does not match the number of people the customer expected to pay for.

## Cause

A seat is consumed by any active or invited member; guests and deactivated members are free.

## Resolution

This article covers the **Enterprise** plan (custom per user per month, unlimited seats included).

1. Settings > Members shows the seat count in the header.
2. Pending invitations consume a seat from the moment they are sent.
3. Removing a member frees the seat at the end of the current period - you are not credited mid-period.
4. Adding a member mid-period is charged prorated to the day.

## Verify

Seats in use, as shown in the header, matches the quantity line on the invoice.

## When to escalate

Escalate when the counts genuinely disagree after pending invites are accounted for.
