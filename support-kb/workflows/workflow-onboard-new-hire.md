# Onboard a new team member

## Who this is for

Workspace owners and admins. Some steps need the owner role, which is noted
where it applies.

## Steps

1. Invite them at Settings > Members with the **member** role; use **guest** for contractors.
2. Add them to the projects they need. Members see all projects, so guests are the right choice for narrow access.
3. Ask them to enrol in MFA on day one - workspace-wide enforcement gives new members only seven days.
4. Point them at the getting-started articles: install, invite, organise, share.

## Verify

They can sign in, see the expected projects, and appear as Active in Settings > Members.

## When to escalate

Escalate when a step cannot be completed because the required control is absent
from your plan, or when the verification above does not hold after completing
every step.
