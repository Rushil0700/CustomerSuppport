# Roll out to a large organisation

## Who this is for

Workspace owners and admins. Some steps need the owner role, which is noted
where it applies.

## Steps

1. Start with one pilot team of 10-20 people for two weeks.
2. Configure SSO and SCIM before widening - retrofitting identity onto existing accounts is far more work.
3. Agree a project naming convention up front; it is the thing organisations most regret not doing.
4. Roll out department by department, not all at once, so support load stays manageable.
5. Schedule the bulk import for a weekend and verify counts before announcing.

## Verify

Each department reaches steady state before the next begins.

## When to escalate

Escalate when a step cannot be completed because the required control is absent
from your plan, or when the verification above does not hold after completing
every step.
