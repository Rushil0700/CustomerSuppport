# Run a quarterly access review

## Who this is for

Workspace owners and admins. Some steps need the owner role, which is noted
where it applies.

## Steps

1. Export the member list and roles from Settings > Members.
2. Question every admin - admin is the role that accumulates without anyone noticing.
3. Review guests: contractors whose engagement ended should be removed, not downgraded.
4. Review API keys by last-used date and revoke anything idle for 90 days.
5. Review share links with no expiry under Settings > Security > Share links.

## Verify

Every remaining admin, guest, key and public link has a current business reason.

## When to escalate

Escalate when a step cannot be completed because the required control is absent
from your plan, or when the verification above does not hold after completing
every step.
