# Cancel a subscription (Enterprise plan)

## Symptom

A customer wants to stop paying.

## Cause

Cancellation stops renewal; it does not delete data.

## Resolution

This article covers the **Enterprise** plan (custom per user per month, unlimited seats included).

1. Settings > Billing > **Cancel subscription**.
2. Choose a reason - it is optional and goes to the product team, not to billing.
3. Confirm. The workspace stays on the paid plan until the period ends, then drops to Free.
4. Export anything above the Free limits before that date.

## Verify

Billing shows 'Cancels on <date>' and no further charges are scheduled.

## When to escalate

Escalate when the customer also wants a refund, or wants the workspace deleted outright.
