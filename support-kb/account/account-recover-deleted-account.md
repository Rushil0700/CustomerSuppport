# Recover a recently deleted account

## Symptom

An account was deleted in error.

## Cause

Deletion disables immediately and purges after 30 days, leaving a recovery window.

## Resolution

1. Contact support from the deleted account's address within 30 days.
2. We verify ownership before restoring anything - expect to answer questions about the account.
3. Restoration returns the account and its workspace memberships.
4. After 30 days the data is genuinely gone and cannot be restored by anyone.

## Verify

Sign-in works again and the expected workspaces are listed.

## When to escalate

Always a human: account restoration is never automated.
